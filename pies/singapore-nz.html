Anatomy of the Singapura Curry Pie

A one-season collaboration: a Singapore-style chicken curry, baked inside a proper New Zealand pastry shell. Scroll to see how the two traditions sit inside one pie.

Scroll to begin

What's in it

  • Shortcrust baseThe standard Golden Crust base — this pie still has to eat like a pie.
  • Curry chickenThigh chicken simmered in a turmeric-spiced curry built the Singapore way, not a British-style korma.
  • Potato & carrotDiced and simmered into the curry until tender, the way you'd find it in a Singapore chicken curry.
  • Curry leaf & fresh chilliStirred through at the end for aromatics and a real kick of heat.
  • Puff pastry lidBrushed with a curry-tinted egg wash — same lamination, warmer colour.
