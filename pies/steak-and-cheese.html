Anatomy of a Steak & Cheese pie

Bigger chunks, thicker gravy. Scroll to see why this one costs a dollar more than its mince cousin.

Scroll to begin

What's in it

  • Shortcrust baseThe same sturdy dough as the mince pie — it has to hold a heavier filling.
  • Diced chuck steakBraised low and slow until fork-tender, kept chunky rather than shredded.
  • Rich beef gravyBuilt straight from the braising liquid — deeper and thicker than the mince pie's gravy.
  • Tasty cheeseA generous layer melted over the steak before the lid goes on.
  • Puff pastry lidLaminated butter pastry, egg-washed and baked until it shatters.
