What's in it

  • Shortcrust baseA sturdier, less laminated dough than the lid — it does the structural work.
  • Beef minceBraised low and slow until it falls apart. No fillers, no mystery cuts.
  • Beef stock gravyThickened with a light roux, built on onion, garlic and black pepper.
  • Tasty cheeseMelted directly onto the mince before the lid goes on, so it sets into the filling.
  • Puff pastry lidLaminated butter pastry, egg-washed and baked until it shatters on the first bite.
