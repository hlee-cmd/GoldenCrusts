Cutie Pie

Our not-so-secret dessert project: a mini pie built for one hand and roughly four bites. Still in the test kitchen — here's what's going into it.

Scroll to see the plan

Small on purpose.

Everything else on the menu is a full-size, savoury, standing-up-and-eating-it pie. Cutie Pie is the opposite on every count: mini, sweet, and built to be finished in the time it takes to order a coffee. Named for the mandarins going into it — the little "cuties" that show up every winter — not just for the size.

We're still finalising the recipe before this one gets its own photography and a full scroll-through anatomy like the rest of the menu. For now, here's the plan.

What's going in it

Still being tested — ingredients and method below, subject to change before this one earns a permanent spot on the menu.

  • Mini shortcrust shellA one-hand base, sized for four bites instead of a full sitting.
  • Mandarin & vanilla bean custard"Cutie" mandarins simmered down and folded through a silky vanilla bean custard.
  • A swirl of lemon curdA bright, tart ribbon run through the custard to stop the sweetness going flat.
  • MascarponeFolded through for a soft, creamy finish rather than a firm-set custard.
  • Cinnamon-sugar dusted lidGolden puff pastry, dusted while still warm so the sugar sets into a light crackle.
  • A heart-shaped steam ventBecause a Cutie Pie without a heart cut into the lid isn't really a Cutie Pie.
