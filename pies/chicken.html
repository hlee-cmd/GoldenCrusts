Anatomy of a Chicken pie

Six layers, one hand, no cutlery. Photographed frame by frame — scroll to pull it apart and put it back together.

What's in it

  • Shortcrust baseSturdier than the lid, because it does the structural work — the whole pie eats standing up.
  • Sautéed mushroomsSliced and cooked down first, sitting at the bottom under a pale, creamy gravy.
  • Peas, carrot & sweetcornDiced fine. The sweetcorn is the giveaway — it's what marks this out from a British chicken pie.
  • Chicken, in chunksTorn pieces of thigh and breast, never mince — loose enough to soak up gravy, big enough to bite into.
  • Creamy chicken gravyBinds the mushrooms, veg and chicken into one filling instead of three separate ones.
  • Puff pastry lidLaminated, egg-washed and baked until it shatters on the first bite.
