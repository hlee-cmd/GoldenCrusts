Anatomy of a Mince & Triple Cheese pie

The everyday mince pie, given three cheeses instead of one. Scroll to see exactly where each one sits — and why the top doesn't just melt, it grills.

Scroll to begin

What's in it

  • Shortcrust baseBuilt sturdier than the lid on purpose — it's what carries a heavier, cheese-loaded filling without ever going soft underneath.
  • Onion, carrot & mushroomFinely diced onion, grated carrot and mushroom, worked through the base of the filling for sweetness and earthiness in every mouthful, not just the top.
  • Seasoned beef mince in thick gravyLean beef mince, browned and seasoned, then braised down in a rich, reduced gravy until the two become one dense, dark layer — no watery pooling.
  • Mozzarella & parmesanMozzarella for stretch and creaminess, parmesan for a salty, nutty edge — the second and third cheese, folded in through the filling itself.
  • Molten aged cheddarA proper aged cheddar, sharp enough to cut through the richness of the mince, melted all the way through rather than left sitting on top.
  • Browned triple-cheese capCheddar, mozzarella and parmesan grated together and grilled directly onto the filling before the lid goes on — blistered and browned like a cheese toastie, not just melted flat.
  • Golden puff pastry lidLaminated butter pastry, egg-washed and baked until it blisters — sealing all three cheeses and the gravy in while it bakes.
