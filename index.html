Built the way a pie should be built.

Shortcrust base for structure, a proper filling that's cooked down low and slow, and a laminated, egg-washed puff pastry lid on top. No shortcuts on the pastry, no mystery-meat mince. Every pie on our menu has a full breakdown page — scroll through the lid, the filling, and the base before you decide what to order.

Five pies live on the menu right now, including a Singapore x New Zealand curry pie we only run for a limited season — plus a mini dessert pie in the test kitchen.
